## Video That Works — For Every Goal, Every Platform

Video is the most powerful content format in digital marketing. It gets more reach, more engagement, more saves, and more conversions than any other format on every major platform. But not all video is the same. The type of video that works depends entirely on your goal, your audience, and where you're showing it.

At Ad Najah, we edit and produce multiple types of videos — each built for a specific purpose and optimized for the platform it lives on.

---

## Types of Videos We Create

### 📊 Infographic Videos

Data and information brought to life through motion. Infographic videos turn complex statistics, processes, or comparisons into clean, engaging animated visuals that are easy to understand and highly shareable.

**Best for:** Explaining how something works, presenting data, step-by-step guides, educational content for social media and LinkedIn.

**What makes them work:** Motion design that guides the eye, clean typography, and visual hierarchy that makes information land in seconds — not paragraphs.

---

### 💰 Sales Videos

Direct-response video designed to convert. A sales video presents your offer clearly, builds desire, handles objections, and drives the viewer to take action — whether that's clicking a link, filling a form, or making a purchase.

**Best for:** Product launches, promotional campaigns, service offers, Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns, landing pages.

**Structure we use:**
- Hook — stop the scroll in the first 2–3 seconds
- Problem — speak to the pain the viewer already feels
- Solution — present your product or service as the answer
- Proof — results, testimonials, or demonstrations that build trust
- CTA — one clear direction for what to do next

---

### 🎥 UGC (User-Generated Content) Style Videos

Organic-looking, creator-style videos that feel native to social media feeds. UGC-style content intentionally avoids the polished, corporate look — because authenticity drives trust, and trust drives conversions.

**Best for:** Meta Ads (UGC ads are among the highest-performing formats on Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Instagram Reels, brand awareness campaigns.

**Why it works:** Audiences have trained themselves to skip obvious ads. UGC-style video bypasses that filter. When an ad looks like content, people actually watch it.

**What we deliver:** Raw footage edited to feel personal and authentic — with captions, natural pacing, and hooks that blend into the feed.

---

### 📦 Product Videos

Showcase-focused videos that put your product front and center. Product videos demonstrate features, highlight benefits, show the product in use, and create desire through visuals alone.

**Best for:** E-commerce brands, product launches, Amazon listings, social media product posts, website hero sections.

**Types of product video we edit:**
- **Demo videos** — showing exactly how the product works
- **Unboxing videos** — the reveal experience, great for building excitement
- **Lifestyle videos** — product in real-life context, creating aspiration
- **Feature highlight** — close-ups and motion that emphasize key product details

---

### 🎬 B-Roll Videos

Supporting footage that adds visual depth, context, and professionalism to any video. B-roll is the behind-the-scenes footage, environment shots, action cuts, and visual transitions that make a video feel cinematic rather than static.

**Best for:** Brand videos, documentary-style content, service showcases, social media storytelling, interview overlays.

**What we do with B-roll:**
- Select and sequence clips for maximum visual impact
- Color grade to achieve a consistent, professional look
- Add transitions, motion cuts, and pacing that keep the viewer engaged
- Sync visuals with music or voiceover for a polished final result

---

### 📱 Reels & Short-Form Video

Fast-paced, algorithm-friendly vertical videos optimized for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Short-form video has the highest organic reach of any content type on all major platforms in 2026.

**Best for:** Brand awareness, going viral, growing followers, driving traffic, repurposing longer content.

**Our editing approach for Reels:**
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds — text overlay, movement, or an unexpected visual
- Fast cuts that maintain energy and momentum
- Captions for sound-off viewing (most Reels are watched without audio)
- Trending audio or brand-consistent music
- CTA at the end — follow, save, click the link

---

### 🎨 Motion Graphics & Animated Creatives

Branded animated content — from logo animations and lower thirds to full animated ad creatives. Motion graphics make your brand look premium, add visual interest to static content, and communicate complex ideas without needing footage.

**Best for:** Ad creatives, brand intros, social media posts, explainer content, presentation videos.

## Our Video Editing Process

**1. Brief**
You share your footage, brand guidelines, goal, and platform. We understand what the video needs to achieve before we touch a single clip.

**2. Edit**
We cut, sequence, color grade, add music, captions, motion graphics, and transitions. Every decision serves the goal.

**3. Review**
You review and give feedback. We revise until the video is exactly right.

**4. Deliver**
Final files delivered in the correct format and resolution for your platform — whether that's 9:16 for Reels, 1:1 for feed, or 16:9 for YouTube and ads.

## Why Video Quality Matters for Ads

For paid advertising specifically, video quality directly impacts your campaign results:

- ✔ **Higher CTR** — Video ads consistently outperform static ads in click-through rate
- ✔ **Lower CPM** — Platforms reward high-engagement creative with lower distribution costs
- ✔ **Better retention** — Well-edited video keeps viewers watching longer, signaling quality to the algorithm
- ✔ **Stronger recall** — Video drives significantly higher brand recall than static content

The difference between a video that converts and one that doesn't is often not the product or the offer — it's the edit.
