## What Are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Ads) are one of the most powerful paid advertising platforms available today. With over 3 billion active users across Facebook and Instagram, Meta gives your business direct access to your ideal customers — by age, location, interest, behavior, and more.

At Ad Najah, we don't just run ads — we build complete Meta ad systems designed to generate consistent leads, drive sales, and grow your brand profitably.

## What We Do

### Audience Research & Strategy
Before we spend a single taka on ads, we deeply research your target audience. We identify who they are, what they want, and where they are in the buying journey. This ensures every campaign is built on solid data — not guesswork.

### Creative Ad Design
We design scroll-stopping visuals, compelling copy, and video ads that capture attention and drive action. Our creatives are A/B tested regularly to find what performs best for your audience.

### Campaign Setup & Launch
We set up your campaigns with the right objective — whether that's traffic, leads, conversions, or brand awareness. We configure targeting, budgets, bidding strategies, and placements for maximum efficiency.

### Optimization & Scaling
Once campaigns are live, we monitor performance daily. We cut what doesn't work and scale what does. This continuous optimization is what separates profitable campaigns from wasted ad spend.

### Reporting & Transparency
You get clear, easy-to-understand reports showing exactly where your money went and what results it generated — ROAS, CPL, CTR, and more.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much budget do I need to start Meta Ads?
We recommend a minimum budget of BDT 15,000–20,000 per month for ad spend to see meaningful results. Our management fee is separate.

### How long before I see results?
Most clients start seeing initial data within the first week. Optimized results typically appear within 30–45 days as we gather data and refine the campaigns.

### Do you handle the creative (images, videos)?
Yes. Our team designs ad creatives tailored to your brand and audience. You provide the product or service details — we handle the rest.
