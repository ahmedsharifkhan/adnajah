## Content Without Strategy Is Just Noise

Posting randomly on social media doesn't build a brand — it wastes time. A strategic content plan aligns every post, story, and reel with your business goals and audience needs. It ensures consistency, maximizes reach, and builds a community around your brand.

At Ad Najah, we create data-backed content plans that give your marketing a clear direction.

---

## What's Included in Our Content Plan

### Audience & Competitor Analysis
We analyze your target audience and study what your competitors are doing — identifying gaps and opportunities for your brand to stand out.

### Monthly Content Calendar
A detailed calendar showing exactly what content to post, on which platform, at what time, and for what purpose. No more guessing what to post next.

### Content Pillars & Themes
We define 4–6 core content pillars (e.g., educational, promotional, testimonials, behind-the-scenes) that guide all your content creation.

### Caption & Messaging Framework
Ready-to-use captions, hooks, and CTAs for each post type — written in your brand voice and optimized for engagement.

### Hashtag Strategy
A curated set of hashtags for each content category, researched for reach and relevance in your industry.

### Campaign Planning
We map out promotional campaigns around product launches, festivals, and seasonal events — ensuring you never miss an opportunity.
