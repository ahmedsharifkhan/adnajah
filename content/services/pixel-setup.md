## Tracking Is the Foundation of Profitable Ads

Running ads without proper tracking is like driving blindfolded. You spend money but have no idea what's working. A correctly installed pixel and event tracking setup gives you the data you need to make smart decisions, optimize campaigns, and scale profitably.

At Ad Najah, we set up and verify your tracking infrastructure so every conversion is measured accurately.

---

## What We Set Up

### Meta Pixel Installation
Complete installation of the Facebook/Meta Pixel on your website. We configure it to fire on all key pages and actions.

### Standard & Custom Event Tracking
We set up standard events (PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, Purchase, Lead, CompleteRegistration) and custom events specific to your business goals.

### Google Tag Manager (GTM) Setup
We install and configure Google Tag Manager as your tag management system — making future tag additions and updates easy without touching code.

### Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
Full GA4 setup including property configuration, data stream, enhanced measurement, and key conversion events.

### Google Ads Conversion Tracking
We connect your Google Ads account to GA4 and configure conversion actions so you know exactly which keywords and ads are driving results.

### Pixel Verification & Testing
We use Meta's Pixel Helper and GTM's Preview Mode to verify every tag fires correctly before going live.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I already have a pixel installed — do I still need this service?
Many pixels are installed incorrectly or missing key events. We audit your existing setup and fix any issues found.

### Do I need a developer for this?
No. We handle the entire technical implementation. We just need access to your website (or GTM container) and ad accounts.

### How long does setup take?
A standard pixel and GTM setup takes 2–3 business days. More complex setups may take up to 5 days.
