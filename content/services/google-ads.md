## Why Google Ads?

When someone searches on Google, they already have intent. They are actively looking for a solution — which means they are far more likely to buy. Google Ads puts your business at the top of search results exactly when your customers are ready to act.

At Ad Najah, we build conversion-focused Google Ads campaigns that attract high-quality traffic and turn clicks into customers.

---

## What We Do

### Keyword Research & Strategy
We identify the exact keywords your customers are searching for. We focus on high-intent, purchase-ready keywords that drive real conversions — not just clicks.

### Campaign Structure & Setup
Proper campaign structure is critical to performance. We build tightly themed ad groups with relevant keywords, compelling ad copy, and optimized landing page alignment.

### Ad Copywriting
We write compelling, benefit-driven ad copy that stands out in search results and compels users to click. We test multiple ad variations to find the highest-performing messages.

### Bid Management & Optimization
We manage bids strategically to maximize your budget. We use smart bidding strategies like Target CPA and Target ROAS to get the best results for your investment.

### Landing Page Recommendations
A great ad sending traffic to a poor landing page wastes money. We provide landing page optimization recommendations to maximize your conversion rate.

### Performance Reporting
Monthly reports showing your impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions, cost-per-click, and ROAS — everything you need to understand your campaign performance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum budget for Google Ads?
We recommend at least BDT 20,000–30,000 per month in ad spend. Results depend on competition in your industry and your target keywords.

### Can Google Ads work for local businesses?
Absolutely. Google Ads is one of the most effective ways to drive local customers to your business. We use location targeting to reach people in your specific area.

### How do you measure success?
We track conversions — leads, calls, form submissions, or purchases — not just clicks. Every campaign is optimized toward your actual business goal.
