## Strategy Breakdown

**Targeting Precision**
- **Job Title** — VP, Director, C-Level, Head of [Department]
- **Company Size** — 50–500 employees (ideal ICP)
- **Industry** — SaaS, Tech, Professional Services
- **Seniority** — Senior, Manager, Director, VP, C-Level only

**Ad Formats Used**
- **Single Image Sponsored Content** — thought leadership and case studies
- **Lead Gen Forms** — pre-filled forms inside LinkedIn (highest CVR)
- **Message Ads (InMail)** — personalized outreach to warm audiences
- **Document Ads** — gated whitepapers and research reports as lead magnets

**Content Strategy**
- Pain-point led headlines ("Still losing deals to...?")
- Social proof: customer logos, case study stats, testimonials
- Clear value exchange: guide → demo → free trial funnel
- Separate creatives for each persona (CEO vs. Marketing Director)

---

## Why LinkedIn Delivered

> **$42 CPL** for qualified B2B decision-makers — the same leads via outbound sales cost $200+.

- Lead Gen Forms converted at **14.3%** (industry avg: 8–10%)
- 31% of leads converted to sales opportunities
- LinkedIn was the **#1 source** of pipeline for 6 consecutive months
