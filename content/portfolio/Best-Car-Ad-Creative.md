## Project Overview

A bold, eye-catching animated ad creative designed for an automotive brand's social media campaign. The goal was to create a scroll-stopping visual that communicated power, speed, and brand prestige — all within the first 2 seconds of viewing.

This type of creative is built specifically for Facebook and Instagram ad placements, where the visual hook is everything. We used motion, contrast, and a strong product focal point to demand attention in a crowded feed.

---

## What We Did

**Creative Direction**
We led with the vehicle as the hero — positioned against a dramatic background to emphasize its design and presence. The animated elements add dynamism without distracting from the product.

**Motion Design**
The animation was crafted to feel high-end and premium, matching the brand's positioning in the market. Smooth transitions and purposeful motion guide the eye toward the vehicle and the call-to-action.

**Ad Format Optimization**
Designed in the optimal square format (1:1) for maximum real estate on both Facebook and Instagram feeds — ensuring the creative looks great across all placements without cropping.
