## Project Overview

A collection of branded social media post designs developed for multiple clients across different industries. The goal: create visually consistent, engaging content that builds brand recognition and drives meaningful interaction.

These designs span promotional posts, educational content, product highlights, and campaign graphics — all built to perform in the social feed.

---

## Design Approach

**Brand Consistency**
Each brand received a custom design system — specific color palettes, typography, and layout styles — applied consistently across all posts. Consistency builds recognition. Recognition builds trust. Trust builds customers.

**Platform Optimization**
Every post was sized and formatted for the specific platform — Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn. Aspect ratios, text placement, and visual weight were adapted for the target environment.

**Content-Driven Design**
We start with the message, not the aesthetics. What does the audience need to understand from this post? That insight shapes the design — not the other way around.
