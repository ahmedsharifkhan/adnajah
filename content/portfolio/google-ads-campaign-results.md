## Strategy Breakdown

**Campaign Architecture**
- **Search Campaigns** — Exact + Phrase match for bottom-funnel keywords
- **Shopping Campaigns** — Product feed optimization for catalog visibility
- **Performance Max** — AI-driven asset groups for cross-channel reach
- **Remarketing** — RLSA audiences targeting past site visitors on Search

**Keyword Strategy**
- Negative keyword lists updated weekly to cut wasted spend
- Competitor bidding campaigns isolated with separate budgets
- Long-tail keyword expansion through Search Term Report mining

**Bid Strategy**
- Smart bidding: Target ROAS with manual overrides during peak seasons
- Dayparting adjustments based on hourly conversion data
- Device bid modifiers: +35% on mobile (highest converting segment)

---

## What Made It Work

> **4.2% CTR** — the industry average for Google Search is 2.1%. We doubled it through tight ad copy relevance and high Quality Scores.

- Ad copy A/B tested every 3 weeks
- Responsive Search Ads (RSA) with 15 headlines, 4 descriptions each
- Sitelink, callout, and structured snippet extensions on every ad
- Landing page CRO aligned with keyword intent
