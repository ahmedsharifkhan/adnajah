## Project Overview

A collection of high-impact animated ad creatives built for social media advertising. These designs were developed with a single purpose: to stop the scroll and communicate the brand's message clearly, boldly, and within the first 2–3 seconds.

Each creative was strategically designed to perform on Facebook, Instagram, and other visual platforms — balancing aesthetic appeal with performance-focused design principles.

---

## What We Did

**Visual Strategy**
We started with the brand's core message and worked backwards — identifying what visual hook would best capture the target audience's attention. Every design choice (color, typography, motion, layout) was made intentionally to serve the campaign goal.

**Animation & Motion**
Dynamic motion elements create visual interest without overwhelming the viewer. The animation guides attention naturally from the hook to the core message to the call-to-action.

**Brand Consistency**
Colors, fonts, and design language aligned with the brand's existing identity — ensuring the ads feel native to the brand while standing out in the social feed.

## Creative Performance Principles

These creatives were designed following proven performance principles:
- ✔ **3-second hook** — Captures attention before the viewer can scroll past
- ✔ **Clear hierarchy** — Brand → Message → CTA, in that order
- ✔ **Motion with purpose** — Animation draws the eye, doesn't distract
- ✔ **Platform-native sizing** — Optimized for Feed (1:1) and Stories (9:16)
- ✔ **Contrast and readability** — Text legible on both light and dark backgrounds
