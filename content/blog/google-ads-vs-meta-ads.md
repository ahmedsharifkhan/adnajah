Two of the most popular paid advertising platforms in the world — Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) — are often compared against each other. Business owners frequently ask: "Which should I use?" The honest answer is: it depends on your goal. But understanding how each platform works will help you make the right decision for your business.

---

## How They Work Differently

The fundamental difference between Google Ads and Meta Ads is **intent**.

**Google Ads = Demand Capture**

When someone types "digital marketing agency in Dhaka" into Google, they have active intent — they are looking for a solution right now. Google Ads put your business in front of these high-intent searchers at the exact moment they are ready to act.

**Meta Ads = Demand Generation**

When someone is scrolling through their Instagram feed, they are not actively searching for anything. Meta Ads interrupt that scroll with compelling content that creates awareness and interest — eventually leading to a purchase decision.

---

## When to Use Google Ads

Google Ads works best when:

- **Your product or service has an existing demand** — People are already searching for what you offer
- **You want immediate leads or sales** — High-intent traffic converts faster
- **You have a local business** — "Restaurant near me," "plumber in Dhaka" — local search ads are highly effective
- **You're selling professional services** — Law firms, accountants, agencies, clinics
- **Your budget is limited** — You only pay when someone clicks (PPC model)

---

## When to Use Meta Ads

Meta Ads works best when:

- **You're building brand awareness** — You want more people to know your brand exists
- **Your product needs to be seen to be desired** — Fashion, home decor, food, beauty
- **You're targeting a specific demographic** — Women aged 25–40, parents, young professionals
- **You have an e-commerce store** — Product discovery happens on social media
- **You want to reach people before they start searching** — Be in their consideration set early

---

## Cost Comparison

There's no universal answer to which is cheaper — it depends on your industry, audience, and competition. However, some general patterns:

| | Google Ads | Meta Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Average CPC (Bangladesh) | BDT 5–50 | BDT 2–20 |
| Competition | Higher in competitive niches | Lower in most niches |
| Conversion rate | Generally higher (intent-based) | Lower but scalable |
| Brand building | Limited | Excellent |

---

## The Best Strategy: Use Both

For most businesses, the ideal approach is to use both platforms — but for different goals:

1. **Meta Ads** for awareness and reaching cold audiences
2. **Google Ads** for capturing people actively searching for your solution
3. **Meta Retargeting** to re-engage website visitors who came from any source

This full-funnel approach ensures you're present at every stage of your customer's journey.

---

## Summary: Which Should You Start With?

| Situation | Recommended Platform |
|---|---|
| Local service business | Google Ads |
| E-commerce store | Meta Ads |
| App or software | Both |
| Professional services | Google Ads |
| Fashion / lifestyle brand | Meta Ads |
| Limited budget, quick results | Google Ads |
| Building brand awareness | Meta Ads |
