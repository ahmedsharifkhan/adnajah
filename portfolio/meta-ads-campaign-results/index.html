Meta Ads — 10.82x ROAS Campaign

Overview

A 12-month Meta Ads engagement for a scaling e-commerce brand. We built a full-funnel paid system — prospecting, nurturing, and retargeting — across Facebook and Instagram simultaneously, with tight creative testing cycles every two weeks.

Strategy Breakdown

Funnel Structure

  • Top of Funnel — Broad + Interest audiences for new reach
  • Middle of Funnel — Video viewers & page engagers retargeting
  • Bottom of Funnel — Cart abandoners, past purchasers (LTV)

Creative Testing

  • 2-week sprint cycles: static vs. carousel vs. video
  • DCO (Dynamic Creative Optimization) for winning combinations
  • Separate creatives per placement (Feed, Reels, Stories)

Technical Setup

  • Meta Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI) for accurate attribution
  • Custom events: ViewContent → AddToCart → InitiateCheckout → Purchase
