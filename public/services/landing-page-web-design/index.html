Landing Page & Web Design

Your Website Is Your First Impression

In 2026, a slow, outdated, or confusing website kills your conversions before they start. Your website is the foundation of all your digital marketing — it’s where ads send traffic, where customers evaluate your brand, and where leads are captured.

At Ad Najah, we design and build high-converting landing pages and websites that look professional, load fast, and turn visitors into customers.

What We Build

High-Converting Landing Pages

Single-page designs built specifically for one goal — a lead, a sale, a sign-up. Every element is designed to guide the visitor toward taking action. Perfect for running paid ad campaigns.

Business Websites

Multi-page professional websites that build credibility, showcase your services, and generate leads 24/7. Fully mobile-responsive and optimized for search engines.

E-Commerce Stores

Online stores designed for seamless shopping experiences. We integrate payment gateways, product pages, and cart functionality.

Portfolio & Agency Sites

Clean, impressive sites that showcase your work and attract clients.

Our Design Principles

  • Speed First — Sites that load in under 2 seconds, optimized for Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile Responsive — Perfect experience on all devices
  • Conversion-Focused — Clear CTAs, strategic layout, and trust signals
  • SEO-Ready — Proper heading structure, meta tags, and clean code
  • Brand Consistent — Aligned with your brand identity

Technology We Use

  • WordPress & Elementor
  • Hugo (Static Sites)
  • HTML / CSS / JavaScript
  • WooCommerce (E-Commerce)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a landing page?

A high-converting landing page typically takes 5–7 business days. A full website takes 2–4 weeks depending on complexity.

Do I need hosting?

We can recommend and set up hosting for you, or build on your existing hosting environment.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. All our websites are fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
