Explainer Video

Tell Your Story in 60 Seconds

An explainer video distills your product or service into a clear, engaging story that your audience understands immediately. Studies show that people are 85% more likely to buy a product after watching a video about it — making explainer videos one of the highest-ROI content investments a business can make.

At Ad Najah, we produce professional explainer videos that communicate your value clearly, build trust, and drive viewers to take action.

What We Produce

Animated Explainer Videos

Motion graphics and 2D animation that bring complex ideas to life. Perfect for SaaS products, apps, services, or any offering that benefits from visual storytelling.

Live-Action Explainer Videos

Real people, real products, real results. Great for building trust and humanizing your brand.

Product Demo Videos

Step-by-step walkthroughs of how your product or service works — ideal for e-commerce, apps, and online tools.

Ad Video Creatives

Short-form (15–30 second) video ads optimized for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. Designed to stop the scroll and drive action.

Our Production Process

  1. Script Writing — We craft a clear, engaging script focused on your audience’s pain point and your solution
  2. Storyboarding — A visual plan of every scene before production begins
  3. Design & Animation — Our creative team brings the storyboard to life
  4. Voiceover & Sound — Professional voiceover and background music
  5. Review & Revisions — Up to 2 rounds of revisions based on your feedback
  6. Final Delivery — Exported in all required formats and resolutions

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Increases conversion rates on landing pages and product pages
  • ✔ Simplifies complex products or services
  • ✔ Builds brand trust and credibility
  • ✔ Performs strongly in paid ad campaigns
  • ✔ Repurposable across all platforms (website, social, YouTube, email)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to produce an explainer video?

A standard animated explainer video takes 10–15 business days from script approval to final delivery.

What length should the video be?

We recommend 60–90 seconds for explainer videos. For social media ads, 15–30 seconds works best.

Do you provide the voiceover?

Yes. We work with professional voiceover artists. You can choose from our available voices or provide your own.
