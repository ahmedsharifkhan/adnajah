Boosting

More Reach. More Visibility. More Results.

Boosting is the fastest way to amplify your best content and reach a wider audience. When done strategically — with the right audience targeting, budget, and creative — boosted posts can deliver impressive reach, engagement, and even conversions at a low cost.

At Ad Najah, we manage your boosting campaigns strategically so every boost reaches the right people and delivers real value.

What We Do

Strategic Post Selection

Not every post should be boosted. We identify your top-performing organic content and select the right posts to boost for maximum return — based on engagement rate, content type, and campaign goal.

Audience Setup

We define your target audience for each boost — by location, age, gender, interests, and behaviors — ensuring your budget reaches the people most likely to engage or convert.

Budget Management

We allocate your boosting budget wisely across posts and time periods to maximize total reach and impact throughout the month.

Creative Optimization

If needed, we optimize the post creative (caption, image, or video) before boosting to improve click-through and engagement rates.

Performance Monitoring

We monitor each boosted post’s performance and make real-time adjustments to maximize results before the campaign ends.

Monthly Reporting

Clear reports showing reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, and cost-per-result for every boosted post.

When Boosting Works Best

  • Product launch announcements
  • Promotional offers and discounts
  • High-performing organic posts
  • Brand awareness campaigns
  • Event promotions
  • Testimonial and social proof posts

Frequently Asked Questions

Is boosting the same as running a Facebook Ad?

Boosting uses Meta’s simplified ad interface directly from a post. It’s less powerful than full Ads Manager campaigns but faster to set up and great for reach and engagement goals.

What’s the minimum budget for boosting?

Even BDT 500–1,000 per post can generate meaningful reach. We recommend a total monthly boosting budget of BDT 5,000 or more for consistent visibility.
